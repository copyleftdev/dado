## What the columns mean

- **Size** is the assembled wall with its bases and caps; depth includes the pull and the latch fingers.
- **Printed** is the mass of the printed parts alone: kernel volume times 1.01 g/cm3 for PA12 nylon.
- **Loaded** adds 110 g a cell for the Pi 5, Active Cooler, PoE HAT, fan and screws. That allowance is assumed.
- **Typical** and **flat out** are power at the switch: 6.2 W and 14.3 W a node, from published Raspberry Pi 5
  measurements with 15 % added for PoE conversion and 0.5 W for the fan. Idle is 3.9 W a node. The flat-out figure is
  also the PoE budget the switches must cover, and every port must be PoE+ (802.3at).
- **Switch ports** is one a Pi and one uplink. An eight-port switch serves seven Pis; a bay holds one such switch.
- **A Jetson** is one Jetson Orin Nano developer kit in each Jetson bay, beside the Pis counted in the Pis column. Each adds
  175 g (NVIDIA's figure for the kit), 15 W typical and 25 W flat out (its two higher power modes), and one
  switch port. It runs from its own 19 V supply, so its power is not part of the PoE budget.
- **Print cost** is printed mass at $0.275 to $0.52 a gram, the range of bureau prices for powder-bed PA12
  collected in November 2026. It covers printing only: no Pis, HATs, fans, switches, screws or finishing.

## What these do not tell you

- Whether the parts print within tolerance. The joints were simulated at +/-0.30 mm; a printed sample is the test.
- Whether a cell stays cool. The 25 mm fan's effect is not modelled, and neither is the Jetson bay's airflow.
- Whether a hundred is sound as drawn. A wall ten high needs a deeper base than the desk-scale one here.
- What a moulded part would cost. These are printing prices at quantities of one to a few hundred.
